LiveWall

Live 4K wallpapers for your Mac.

Turn any video or photo into a desktop wallpaper that loops flawlessly across every display, and is engineered to barely touch your battery.

Download for Mac View on GitHub

Requires macOS 14 or later. Apple Silicon optimized.

LiveWall — Gallery

Everything a wallpaper should be

Native Swift, AppKit and AVFoundation. Zero third-party dependencies. It lives in your menu bar with no Dock icon.

Any video, any photo

Play MP4, MOV or M4V video (HEVC recommended), or set a PNG, JPEG or HEIC image. Import by drag-and-drop into the gallery.

Battery-smart by design

Auto-pauses on lock, display sleep, full occlusion, Low Power Mode, and optionally on battery. It never keeps your display awake.

Native and tiny

One shared hardware decode feeds every display through a single AVQueuePlayer, with gapless looping and muted playback.

Built to sip power

A live wallpaper should never be the reason your fans spin up. LiveWall gates playback behind five conditions and drops decoding the moment any one of them is true.

  • 1 Screen is locked
  • 2 Display is asleep
  • 3 Wallpaper is fully occluded
  • 4 Low Power Mode is on
  • 5 Running on battery (toggleable)

How it works

Wallpaper windows render one level below the desktop icons, so your video sits behind everything on screen. Static images skip the engine entirely and use Apple's native wallpaper API, so nothing runs at all.

One honest install note

LiveWall is ad-hoc signed but not notarized, so macOS Gatekeeper blocks the very first launch. It is safe, and the fix takes one step:

  1. Download the DMG and drag LiveWall into Applications.
  2. Right-click (or Control-click) LiveWall.app and choose Open, then confirm.

Prefer the terminal? Clear the quarantine flag instead:

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/LiveWall.app
